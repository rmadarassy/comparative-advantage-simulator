5 · Check your understanding

At the original productivities, what is the opportunity cost of 1 kg of cheese in France?

Show exam review explanations

France has absolute and comparative advantage in cheese; Germany has absolute and comparative advantage in cars. France's autarky price of cheese is ⅛ car/kg; Germany's is ½ car/kg. Mutually beneficial trade prices lie strictly between these opportunity costs. The whiteboard illustrates France producing 8,000 kg cheese, exporting 3,000 kg and importing 600 cars at 5 kg per car.

In this Ricardian example, trade expands France's consumption possibilities when the world price differs favorably from its domestic opportunity cost. A particular consumption bundle need not contain more of both goods; compare the plotted trade bundle with a feasible no-trade bundle.
